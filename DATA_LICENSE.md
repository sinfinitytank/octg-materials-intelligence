# Source data and attribution

The project uses the four files supplied by the project owner, preserved byte-for-byte under `data/source/`. The supplied CSV is the initial record authority. Normalized JSON preserves all source values and source links. Inclusion in this public project follows the owner's explicit publication request; it does not establish ownership of third-party data or grant a new data license.

The MIT software license does **not** apply to source files, manufacturer information, standards text, trademarks or the underlying compiled material records. Redistribution rights for this compilation have not been independently established. Contributors and downstream users must determine their rights for their intended use. Do not upload confidential supplier quotations or controlled standards documents.

Manufacturer pages and official standards resources are linked for traceability. The application does not copy paid standards or claim that catalogue descriptions establish qualification. The original standalone HTML contains an embedded compilation; that original remains unchanged for audit purposes. No manufacturer PDF or paid standard is bundled by this implementation.

The six source price ranges are historical, indicative China FOB market benchmarks, without complete quotation metadata. They are retained as supplied and excluded from automatic costing. Local user quotations remain in the user's browser and are never uploaded by the application.

See [source audit](docs/SOURCE_AUDIT.md) and [data model](docs/DATA_MODEL.md) for exact provenance, source discrepancies and normalization rules.
