How the decision logic works

Hard screen: a published SMYS below your requirement is a mechanical mismatch. Unknown SMYS is flagged instead of treated as compliant. Cost-tier filters act as procurement preferences, not material suitability.

Soft rank: textual sour-service claims, CO₂-service alloy families and marked high-collapse variants increase catalog relevance. Chloride, temperature, pH and sulfur trigger qualification cautions—not invented pass/fail thresholds.

Required before specifying: NACE MR0175 / ISO 15156-2 or -3 limits or qualification; actual corrosion calculations and localized-corrosion envelope; supplier availability; applicable API 5CT/5CRA edition; OD/wall-dependent collapse/burst/axial ratings, connection and load cases.

Original sources: links appear per record. Standards: ISO 15156 · ISO/TR 10400 · API latest updates.
